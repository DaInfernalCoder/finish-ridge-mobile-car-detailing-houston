# Finish Ridge design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile car detailing for Houston drivers who need a clear way to describe current vehicle condition and choose interior, exterior, or complete care. The page should reduce uncertainty and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Ridge navy — `#173044`
- Sandstone — `#D4B27D`
- Sky wash — `#C8E4EA`
- Asphalt — `#4B565B`
- Cloud — `#F8FAF8`
- Display: Barlow Condensed with Arial Narrow fallback
- Body: Manrope with Trebuchet fallback

## Layout thesis

Use a rising condition profile to make the path from assessment to requested service scope and final review easy to follow.

## Signature

One restrained ridge line maps the four inspection zones without implying a guaranteed correction outcome.

## Critique

The inherited temperature motif belonged to the prior brand. The revision removes ember language and uses a practical condition profile, with sandstone reserved for action points and ridge markers.
